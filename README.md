# Nourish

一个小小的私人藏书馆，收录了一些喜欢的书籍，电影，戏剧，播客和游戏。目前只包含自己还未完整体验过的作品。希望你喜欢。

A small personal archive of books, films, and things that linger. Hope you find some small surprise encounters here.

目前仍是拙劣粗糙的自用版本，请尽情探索编辑，构筑属于自己的藏书间吧。

This is a crude preview of my personal gallery, you're warmly invited to make your version by adding beautiful pieces you'd like to keep, give them your own scents, notes and more...

## How to use (AI写的，随便看看)

1. 打开网页后，可以直接搜索、按类型浏览，或通过气味标签探索作品。  
   Open the page to search, browse by category, or explore through scent tags.

2. 想添加自己的条目时，在顶部填写名称；英文名、分类和气味都可以留空。  
   To add something of your own, fill in its name at the top. English name, category, and scent are optional.

3. 添加后，你可以点开卡片中的“编辑”，补充年份、创作者、图片和自己的推荐语。  
   After adding it, open “edit” on the card to add the year, creator, image, and your own note.

4. 所有修改默认保存在你自己的浏览器里，不会影响原始清单或其他访客。  
   Changes are saved in your own browser by default, so they do not affect the original list or anyone else’s.
